Gochiso Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026 · Developer: Gabriel Mckenzie · Contact: thefilthygarments@gmail.com

Gochiso does not collect any data. That is the whole policy, but here is the detail App Store review and anyone curious will want.

What we collect

Nothing. Gochiso has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Where your progress is stored

Your campaign is stored on your device only, in the app’s own storage. That is the level you have reached, your ¥ balance, the grades you have earned, the upgrades you own, and your sound, haptics and motion preferences.

It is never uploaded, never backed up to us, and never shared with anyone. If you delete the app, it is gone. You can also erase it yourself at any time from Settings → Reset Progress inside the game.

Network access

Gochiso contains no networking code. The app cannot send or receive data over the internet, because there is nothing in it that can open a connection. There is no server behind this game.

Tracking

Gochiso does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking domains and no collected data types, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Children

Gochiso is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either. There is no chat, no user-generated content, and no way to communicate with other players.

Purchases

There are none. The in-game shop spends ¥ earned by playing. Gochiso has no in-app purchases and no way to spend real money.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gochiso ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
