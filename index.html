BentoRush — Support

Developer: Gabriel Mckenzie · Last updated: 15 August 2026

BentoRush is a merge puzzle with a dinner service attached. Merge dishes up the ladder, read the order queue, and get every order out before patience runs out.

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion?
Email thefilthygarments@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who made the game.

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the level number you were on.

Common questions

How do I merge?
Tap or drag one dish onto another of the same kind. Two of a dish make the next one up, all the way through eight dishes from rice to a full nabe.
My prep station is full and nothing can merge.
Use a rescue — it clears the dead weight off the station. If you run out of rescues while the board is locked, the shift ends as a kitchen jam, which is a different ending from losing your guests.
I ran out of space. Should I stop restocking?
No. Supply is free and unlimited, and the board starves without it. Managing when to restock is the core skill of the game.
What do the B, A and S grades mean?
B is a service complete, A is a clean shift with nobody lost, and S is a flawless service — cleared, clean, and holding a combo. A shift you lose is left ungraded rather than stamped with a bad mark.
Are there in-app purchases?
No. The shop spends ¥ you earn by playing. There is no way to spend real money in BentoRush, and there are no ads.
How do I turn off the sound, haptics, or animation?
All three are in Settings, reachable from the title screen. Sound also obeys your iPhone’s ring/silent switch and mixes with your own music, so it will never interrupt a podcast. The motion toggle is separate from the system Reduce Motion setting, which BentoRush also respects.
How do I replay the tutorial?
Settings → Replay Tutorial. It will run again on your next shift.
How do I erase my progress?
Settings → Reset Progress, then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone.
I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?
Not currently. BentoRush stores your campaign on the device with no account and no server, which is what lets it collect nothing about you. Your progress moves only if your new phone is restored from an encrypted backup of the old one.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone running iOS 17 or later, in portrait. It runs at 120 Hz on ProMotion displays.

Privacy

BentoRush collects nothing, has no accounts, and contains no networking code at all. Read the full Privacy Policy.
