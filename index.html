Gochiso Support

Developer: Gabriel Mckenzie · Last updated: 29 September 2026

Gochiso is a merge puzzle with a dinner service attached. Merge dishes up the ladder, read the order queue, and get every order out before patience runs out.

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion?
Email thefilthygarments@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who made the game.

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the level number you were on.

Common questions

How do I merge?
Drag one dish onto another of the same kind. Two of a dish make the next one up, all the way through eight dishes from rice to a full nabe. Dragging is the whole job. There is nothing to tap.
Where does the food come from?
The walk in. It holds a set amount and you pull from it when you want it, rather than waiting on a clock. Sending orders out puts stock back. Serving feeds the kitchen and hoarding starves it, and that is the skill.
My board is full and nothing can merge.
Use a rescue. It clears some dishes off the board. If you run out of rescues while the board is stuck, the level ends as board full.
What do the stars mean?
One star clears a level. Two is a shift without a single walkout. Three is that while holding a combo. One star is always enough to move on, and a shift you lose is left unstarred rather than marked. The letter beside them is the same grade written B, A or S.
Some levels have no clock and nobody waiting.
Those are prep puzzles. Every fourth level from the seventh is one: a fixed board, a move budget, and no queue. Every puzzle can be solved in the number of moves it advertises.
What happens after level 100?
The chain posts you abroad. Twelve branches across eight countries, twenty levels each. Same menu and same counter. What changes is the room and the people who come through the door.
Can I play without the clock?
Yes. Turn on Relaxed Mode in Settings. There is no timer and nobody leaves. Levels still unlock and still pay. The best grade in Relaxed Mode is A.
Are there in-app purchases?
No. The shop spends ¥ you earn by playing. There is no way to spend real money in Gochiso, and there are no ads.
How do I turn off the sound, haptics, or animation?
All three are in Settings, reachable from the title screen. Sound obeys your iPhone’s ring switch and mixes with your own music, so it will never interrupt a podcast. The motion toggle is separate from the system Reduce Motion setting, which Gochiso also respects.
How do I see the lesson again?
Settings → HOW TO PLAY. It is on the pause card during a shift as well.
How do I erase my progress?
Settings → RESET PROGRESS, then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone.
I got a new phone. Can I move my progress?
Not currently. Gochiso stores your campaign on the device with no account and no server, which is what lets it collect nothing about you. Your progress moves only if your new phone is restored from an encrypted backup of the old one.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone running iOS 17 or later, in portrait. It runs at 120 Hz on ProMotion displays.
